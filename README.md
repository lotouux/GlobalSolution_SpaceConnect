## About the Project
**ORION** is an innovative **Space-SaaS (Software as a Service for Space)** designed to orchestrate Artificial Intelligence workloads across Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite constellations. 

As the terrestrial infrastructure faces an imminent energy crisis due to the massive expansion of generative AI, ORION provides the software backbone required to enable **Orbital Data Centers**. By shifting computational loads to the vacuum of space, utilizing limitless solar energy and passive cooling, ORION mitigates terrestrial grid exhaustion while establishing a highly resilient, interstellar communication network.

---

## Project Goal
The **Orion Control Center** serves as the terrestrial Digital Twin of the orbital mesh. It is designed to empower space agencies, governments, and enterprise clients with a B2B interface that translates highly complex aerospace operations into actionable intelligence. 

Our system guarantees zero data loss and maximum bandwidth efficiency by combining:
- **Edge AI & Semantic Caching** in orbit.
- **FSOC (Free-Space Optical Communications)** via laser links.
- **DTN (Delay/Disruption Tolerant Networking)** to survive solar storms and orbital eclipses.

## Features Implemented

### 🌍 1. Atmospheric Digital Twin (Orbital Mesh)
- **Real-Time 3D Monitoring:** Visualizes the active satellite constellation and optical laser links.
- **Energy-Aware Tracking:** Distinct indicators for nodes operating under direct sunlight versus those in orbital eclipse.
- **Dynamic Stress Testing:** Includes a "Simulate Solar Storm" protocol to visually demonstrate network degradation and self-healing routing.

### 🛰️ 2. Edge AI & Hardware Telemetry
- **Node-Level Health Monitoring:** Displays real-time SoC (State of Charge), GPU temperature, and AI Workload status for individual satellites (e.g., SAT-031).
- **Proactive Workload Migration:** Demonstrates autonomous decision-making where a node entering the night side of Earth transfers its heavy AI processing tasks to a sunlit neighboring node via laser.
- **Protocol Transparency:** Displays the active use of Bundle Protocol v7 (DTN) and 100 Gbps optical link capacities.

### 🧠 3. Semantic Caching & Optimization
- **Data Deduplication:** Uses AI-driven cosine similarity to compare mathematical vectors (embeddings) instead of raw images.
- **Bandwidth Economy:** If an anomaly (e.g., a forest fire) is already known, the satellite retrieves the data from the orbital cache rather than wasting expensive downlink bandwidth.
- **Business Intelligence Dashboard:** Translates saved bandwidth into tangible metrics (Terabytes and Millions of USD saved).
- **Customizable Thresholds:** Features a slider to adjust the aggressiveness of the Semantic Engine.

### 🚨 4. Semantic Alerts Center (Data-to-Insight)
- **Payload Delivery Verification:** The ultimate proof of the DTN architecture, showing that critical alerts survived orbital interruptions.
- **Geospatial Mapping:** Plots identified anomalies (Fires, Gas Leaks, Floods, Storms) across the globe based on edge inferences from various nodes.
- **Raw JSON Inspector:** Allows technical operators to view the exact decoded mathematical vector, node ID, latency, and confidence score sent by the satellite.
- **Categorized Feed:** Real-time chronological feed of validated events.
